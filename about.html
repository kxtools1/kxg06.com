Built Around Industrial Supply

CROSSORDER Industrial Supply is the international-facing industrial supply and sourcing identity of 跨序电子商务有限公司, serving B2B buyers looking to source industrial products from China.

Our Background

From local industrial distribution experience to global sourcing.

CROSSORDER is supported by hands-on experience in China's hardware and electromechanical supply market. Our background includes offline industrial distribution and supplier-network experience associated with Tianbang Hardware & Electromechanical.

For international customers, CROSSORDER is the unified business identity used to coordinate industrial product sourcing, supplier consolidation, OEM requirements and export support.

Our approach

We focus on practical industrial procurement: understand the requirement, organize suitable supply options, reduce fragmented supplier communication and help buyers move from RFQ to shipment more efficiently.

Broad Category Thinking

We are not limited to a single factory product line. We work around the buyer's purchasing list.

B2B Focus

Long-Term Supply

Build repeatable sourcing and purchasing workflows rather than one-off product introductions.
