ABOUT US

Industrial supply support from China to global markets.

Industrial Supply from China, Built Around Your Needs

CROSSORDER is a China-based business combining industrial product distribution with industrial consumables supply and manufacturing. We focus on international B2B purchasing, helping buyers source tools, supplies and consumables for resale, production, maintenance and project work.

Our aim is to make purchasing across different industrial categories more straightforward, with clear communication about what you need and how each product will be used.

A Broad Range of Industrial Products

Our product scope includes hand tools, power tools, cutting tools, electrical supplies, pneumatic and hydraulic products, safety products and industrial consumables. Buyers can enquire about a single item or share a purchasing list covering several categories.

If a product is not shown on our website, send its name, model, photograph or specification so we can review your requirement.

For Distributors and Industrial Buyers

We welcome enquiries from importers, distributors, wholesalers, retailers, factories and project purchasing teams worldwide. Your requirements may involve extending a product range, replenishing frequently used consumables or sourcing items for a specific application.

Tell us your intended use, quantities and destination market so the discussion reflects your business needs.

Clear Specifications Before a Quotation

A useful quotation starts with the details: product specifications, materials, quantities, packaging and delivery requirements. For OEM, private-label or other customization enquiries, share your branding, packaging or technical requirements for review.

Product suitability, customization options, testing requirements, pricing and delivery arrangements should be agreed for each order.

A Practical Approach to Long-Term Cooperation

We value clear requirements, careful product matching and realistic commitments. Our goal is to build lasting business relationships through responsive communication and a shared understanding of each order.

Whether you are exploring a first purchase or planning repeat orders, we welcome a direct conversation about your requirements.
