Industrial Sourcing FAQ

Practical answers for distributors, wholesalers, factories and project buyers sourcing industrial products from China.

Can I send one RFQ containing many different industrial products?

Yes. Multi-category RFQs are central to our sourcing model. You can send an Excel list, BOM, model list, photos or written requirements covering multiple categories.

What information should I include in an RFQ?

Include product name, model or reference brand, specifications, material, quantity, destination and any packaging, OEM, inspection or certification requirements. Photos and drawings are useful when identification is difficult.

Can you source products that are not shown on the website?

Yes. The website represents core categories, not the complete sourcing range. Send the product information and we can evaluate available supply options.

Can different products be combined into one order or shipment?

Mixed purchasing and supplier consolidation can be coordinated when the products, suppliers, quantities, packing and shipment plan are commercially practical.

Do you support OEM or private-label products?

Where the product and manufacturer allow it, we can coordinate logo application, labels, color requirements, packaging and other agreed customization.

Is there one MOQ for all products?

No. MOQ depends on the product, specification, packaging, customization and manufacturer. We review MOQ item by item during quotation.

Can I request samples before a bulk order?

Sample availability and cost depend on the product and supplier. For products where sampling is practical, sample requirements can be discussed before mass ordering.

How is product quality handled?

Quality requirements should be defined before order confirmation. Depending on the product and agreement, checks can include specification, quantity, appearance, packaging and other agreed inspection points.

Can you source according to an existing brand or model reference?

Yes. A reference brand/model can help clarify the required function and specification. We will distinguish between sourcing the original branded product and sourcing an equivalent or alternative product.

What markets can you ship to?

Export feasibility depends on product type, destination-country requirements, transport restrictions and the agreed trade terms. Destination information should be included in the RFQ so shipping requirements can be reviewed.
